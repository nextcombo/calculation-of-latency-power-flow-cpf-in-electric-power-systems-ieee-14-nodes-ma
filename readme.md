1. Overview of Power System Flow Calculations
The flow calculations in power systems are of significant reference value for the preliminary planning and operational optimization of power grids. This paper aims to conduct flow calculations on complex power networks using MATLAB, comparing three methods: Newton-Raphson method, PQ fast decomposition method, and DC flow method. The comparison and discussion will focus on aspects such as calculation accuracy and algorithm complexity. Flow calculations in power systems serve as a means for static analysis of power system networks. They can determine the voltage magnitude, phase angle, and power at various nodes of the power grid by analyzing the given network structure and partial node operating conditions. This allows for the determination of power distribution within the grid, network losses, and economic viability of the power grid. Therefore, flow calculations play a crucial role in power system analysis. There is extensive research conducted both domestically and internationally on flow calculations.
